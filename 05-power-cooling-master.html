3. UPS Topologies & Generator Failover Continuity

  • Online Double-Conversion UPS: Continuously converts incoming AC → DC → clean pure sine-wave AC with 0 ms transfer time. Mandatory for enterprise server racks.
  • UPS Battery Trap: Degraded VRLA batteries still read "100% Charge" at idle (float voltage), then collapse within seconds under real load due to high internal resistance. Load-test and replace every 3–5 years.
  • Generator Slew Rate vs. UPS: Unloaded diesel generators can drift in frequency (e.g., 57–63 Hz), causing double-conversion UPS units to reject generator input and drain batteries unless generator governors or UPS input windows are tuned.
